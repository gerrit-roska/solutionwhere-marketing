To keep a Professional classroom teaching or school leader certificate, a Teaching Assistant Level III certificate, or a Professional Teaching Assistant certificate valid while practicing in a New York public school, BOCES, or applicable nonpublic school, the educator must complete 100 clock hours of Continuing Teacher and Leader Education (CTLE) in each five-year registration period and keep an active registration in TEACH.

## Who must complete CTLE and register

Registration and CTLE are separate obligations, and they do not apply to every certificate.

Educators must register in TEACH every five years if they hold a Permanent or Professional certificate in classroom teaching or educational leadership, a Level III Teaching Assistant certificate, or a Professional Teaching Assistant certificate, and they practice in an applicable school. Practicing means employed 90 days or more in a school year by a single New York district, BOCES, or applicable nonpublic school.

CTLE applies to holders of a Professional classroom teaching or educational leadership certificate, a Level III Teaching Assistant certificate, or a Professional Teaching Assistant certificate. Permanent certificates alone do not carry a CTLE requirement. Pupil personnel certificates (school counselor, school psychologist, school social worker, school attendance teacher) and Teaching Assistant Level I or Level II certificates are not subject to registration or CTLE.

There is one registration period per educator, and one CTLE requirement per educator, no matter how many certificates they hold. Hours completed for one Professional title cover the others.

Educators who are not practicing in an applicable school register as Inactive and are not subject to CTLE while in that status. They must change to Registered Active before they practice again.

## Hours, language acquisition, and what counts

Educators who practice in an applicable school for the full five-year registration period complete 100 clock hours of acceptable CTLE. Hours cannot be carried from a prior period. One CTLE clock hour is at least 60 minutes of instruction from a NYSED-approved CTLE sponsor. A semester hour of credit from an approved sponsor equals 15 clock hours. A quarter hour equals 10.

If the educator is Inactive for part of the period, they complete 20 clock hours for each year they were practicing in an applicable school. That rate is not prorated for a partial year.

Acceptable CTLE is content of a certificate held, pedagogy, or language acquisition for English language learners. Ordinary professional development that a sponsor has not designated as CTLE does not count.

Language acquisition is a share of the 100 hours, not an extra stack:

- Professional ESOL or Bilingual Education holders: at least 50 percent of required hours in language acquisition aligned to the content they teach.
- Other Professional teachers, school building leaders, school district leaders, and Teaching Assistant Level III holders: at least 15 percent.
- School district business leaders: at least 15 percent dedicated to the needs of ELLs and related mandates.

A district with fewer than 30 ELLs enrolled, or with ELLs under 5 percent of enrollment, can obtain an exemption from the language acquisition share. The educator still completes the full CTLE hour total.

Mentoring may count, at the district's or BOCES' discretion if it is an approved sponsor: up to 30 hours for mentoring a new classroom teacher, and up to 25 hours for mentoring a teacher candidate, in each five-year period.

National Board Certification meets the CTLE hour requirement in the registration period it is achieved, provided the language acquisition requirement is also met.

## How registration works in TEACH

First-time Professional and Teaching Assistant Level III certificates issued on or after July 1, 2016 are automatically registered for the first five-year period. The period runs from issuance until the last day of the month before the educator's birth month in the fifth year.

TEACH sends reminder email at six months, three months, and one month before the registration expires. Educators re-register beginning six months before that date. At re-registration they attest in TEACH that they completed the required CTLE. They do not upload transcripts or certificates unless NYSED asks.

Educators keep their own CTLE records for at least three years after the registration period ends: activity title, hours, split among content, pedagogy, and language acquisition, approved sponsor name and number, dates, location, and attendance. NYSED may request that file. Summaries from a learning management system are not accepted as proof if the Department asks. Approved sponsors, including districts, keep award records for eight years and issue a completion certificate.

A published five-year registration fee is not listed on NYSED's certificate fee schedule. Initial Reissuance applications are $50. Educators who willfully do not register, or do not mark Inactive when they stop practicing, may be subject to moral character review.

Educators who have not finished CTLE by the end of the period may request conditional registration, which adds one year to complete the hours. Conditional registration is not renewed except for good cause.

## Initial certificates: advancement and reissuance

Initial classroom teacher, school building leader, and school counselor certificates are valid for five years. They are not renewed through CTLE.

Advancement to Professional requires the education and experience the title demands, including three years of acceptable experience with one year mentored. Teachers need an acceptable master's in the certificate content or a related area, or a master's in any field plus 12 graduate semester hours in the content core or a related area.

Holders who have not met Professional requirements may apply for up to two Initial Reissuances. Each reissuance is valid for five years from its effective date. Apply no more than six months before the Initial expires. The application fee is $50. The educator submits an attestation form to the Office of Teaching Initiatives and applies in TEACH. Eligibility differs for the first and second reissuance. After two reissuances, further extensions are not granted.

CTLE completed on an Initial title does not extend that Initial. An educator who also holds a Professional certificate still completes CTLE for the Professional.

## Step-by-step: register and attest

1. Confirm in TEACH whether you are Registered Active, Registered Inactive, or approaching the end of a five-year period.
2. Complete CTLE only with NYSED-approved sponsors. Keep each completion certificate.
3. Track language acquisition hours separately so the 15 percent or 50 percent share is visible.
4. Beginning six months before the registration expiration date, open re-registration in TEACH.
5. Attest that you completed the required hours. Do not send documents unless NYSED requests them.
6. If hours are short, request conditional registration instead of attesting to hours you did not complete.
7. Update name, address, and a working personal email in TEACH within 30 days of a change, so reminder notices arrive.

## Where districts get New York CTLE tracking wrong

Districts still think hours report into TEACH the way the old professional development requirement did. After the 2015-16 school year, districts and BOCES no longer report those hours in TEACH. The educator attests. The district, if it is an approved sponsor, must still issue completion certificates and keep award records for eight years.

HR watches hire dates. The PD office approves workshops. Nobody checks that the workshop was designated CTLE by an approved sponsor, so staff collect hours that will not survive an audit.

Districts tell Initial holders to get their CTLE in as if that renews the Initial. It does not.

Expiration of the registration period is tied to birth month, not to the date printed on an old certificate document. Districts that only watch certificate issue dates miss the TEACH registration date.

When an educator transfers, the receiving district cannot see the prior CTLE file unless the educator kept it. There is no statewide transcript the new employer can pull.

Language acquisition is treated as optional extra training. It is a required share of the 100 hours unless the district has an approved exemption.
